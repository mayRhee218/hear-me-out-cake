Privacy policy

Your photos and downloads

The cake editor processes your selected photos in an isolated browser frame, separate from the page that displays ads. The editor keeps its copies of your photos on your device and uses them to create previews and toppers. When you download a cake, your browser generates the PNG on your device.

Advertising

Google AdSense and its advertising partners may place or read cookies, use web beacons, and process IP addresses or other identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where your choices allow personalized advertising, ads may be based on visits to this site and other sites. How Google uses information from sites and apps.

Your privacy choices

Use the site's consent controls when available to review or change your ad privacy choices. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings and adjust Google ad personalization in My Ad Center.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages serves the website files. When you visit the site, GitHub may receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser details. GitHub's privacy statement.
